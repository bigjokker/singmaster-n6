3. I_{p,k} and q(k)

p = k+g prime ⇒ k ≡ -g (mod p)
  C(k+j,j) ≡ (-1)^j C(g-1,j) (mod p)   for 0 <= j < g
  NOT C(k,j). ChatGPT boxed C(k,j) once; that identity is false.
  I_{p,k} = {0} ∪ {(-1)^j C(g-1,j) mod p}

8. Known engine caveats (do not forget)
---------------------------------------
  Nearby negatives are not complete certificates.
    nearby_solutions samples n geometrically, bisects sign changes, and
    stops at n <= 40k+64. Degree d+e (up to 12) can hide roots. Honest
    claim: no sampled/bracketed root passed the exact check.
    The small-box sampler check passed; that does not prove k=1e9.

  Digit labels can be off by one.
    int(bit_length * log10(2))+1 overcounts just below a power of ten.
    Scan starts (min_m_for_digits) are exact. pair_is_past_2017 and
    value_digits in JSON are labels only.

  Intersect k_extra is a window, not "no extra representation exists."
    i=8 k<=120 is done. D is k<=300, not a proof.
    i=9 k<=5 is done. E is k<=12, not a proof.

  Collide (3,5) and (4,5) were skipped in B. Hard skip at m > 1e11.

  collide JSON past_2017_only is false even on frontier scans.
    Judge those files by m_range and value_digits, not that flag.

  Classifier is already fixed. Do not "fix the adjacent-row classifier"
  again and do not redo nearby A because of it.
